# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Axelliant engineers work in each other's code and in our clients' code every day. We hold
ourselves to a simple standard of professional behaviour:

- **Review the change, not the person.** "This query holds a lock across a network call" is a
  review. "Did you even test this?" is not.
- **Assume the other person had a reason.** Ask for it before replacing it.
- **Say plainly when something is wrong** — including your own work. Hiding a defect to avoid an
  awkward conversation costs the client more than the conversation would have.
- **Credit the people who did the work.**
- **Protect client confidentiality.** A client's code, data and business are theirs. They are not
  discussed outside the engagement, not pasted into public tools, and not used as examples in
  shared material without permission.

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in any channel — repositories,
issues, pull requests, chat or meetings.

## Reporting

Report a concern to your engineering lead or to HR. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody
is penalised for raising one in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in every Axelliant repository, every client repository we contribute to, and
every channel where Axelliant engineers work together.
